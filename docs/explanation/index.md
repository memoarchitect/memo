# Explanation

The introduction to MEMO is organized around two questions:

## Why MEMO

[Why MEMO](../why/index.md) explains the engineering problem: medical-device
assurance depends on a connected argument, while design changes can leave
requirements, architecture, risk, verification, and evidence describing
different baselines.

## What is MEMO

[What is MEMO](../what/index.md) explains the ontology: its scenario-driven
foundation, architecture layers, assurance disciplines, functions and typed
relationships, model viewpoints and views, and methodology selection.

Read [Why MEMO](../why/index.md) first, then continue to
[What is MEMO](../what/index.md).
